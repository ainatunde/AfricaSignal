Editorial insight drafts

New assessments can prompt a private AI draft when draft-only synthesis is enabled in Language model settings. Every draft stays attached to the assessment version and its evidence. Approval records an editor's review only. A separate ‘approve and queue email’ action sends to verified followers who opted into reviewed insight emails; it does not publish or post to X.

Evidence-linked insight candidates

These private opportunities are selected deterministically from current eligible assessments. Comparison packets require the same topic, exact place, month-on-month measure, unit, and period. A candidate’s selection is an editorial choice only; it does not approve, email, publish, or post anything.

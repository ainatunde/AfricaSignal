Policy situations

A policy series follows one regulator's rate, such as a tariff or a regulated price. Each series gets one situation per place it covers. A situation shows nothing until a primary document (a regulator order or notice) for the series has been read; news reports alone never make a figure public. The series in config/policies.yaml are listed here but can only be changed in that file. Series you add here take effect at once: the language model is offered the code, the NERC reader accepts it and its situations are created.

Alerts

Raised by the hourly backup check, five-minute public-page and object-storage checks, and every-minute operational Guardian check. Incidents cover source failures, scheduler and queue progress, monthly-series reference age, delivery lag, unresolved email and social outcomes, model spend, retention, storage, backups, restore drills, and public-page failures. A recognized transient collection outage can receive one delayed, permission-checked retry per source recovery interval; parser and delivery failures require an operator. Already delivered items affected by invalidation appear in Delivered item corrections. An incident closes when a later check confirms recovery. No external alert recipient is configured; review this page or the audit log.

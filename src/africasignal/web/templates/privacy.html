Privacy notice

{{ draft_banner("notice") }}

AfricaSignal is built to work with as little information about you as possible. You can read everything on the site without an account. This page says what we do collect, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and how to get it deleted.

Who is responsible

{{ operator() }} runs AfricaSignal and decides how your information is used (in data protection terms, the data controller). You can reach us at {{ contact() }}. This notice is written with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

The short version

  • Our software does not store your IP address, your browser details or the full address of the pages you visit. (The companies that host the site may keep short technical logs; see “Technical logs” below.)
  • We do not use advertising or third-party analytics, and the site loads no scripts, fonts or images from other companies.
  • An account needs only your email address. The weekly email is off until you turn it on.
  • When you use “Use my location”, your position is turned into a state and local government area and then thrown away.
  • You can download your data and delete your account yourself, at any time, on the account page.

What we collect

When you just read the site

  • A visitor code. The first time you open a page we put a random code in a cookie ({{ anon_cookie }}). It is not made from anything about you. It lets us count how many people visit and how many come back, and limit feedback to {{ feedback_limit }} submissions per visitor per day.
  • Page-view records. For each page you open we record the time, the kind of page (for example “situation” or “coverage”), which situation it was if it was a situation page, and where you came from if the link ended in ?ref=wa, x, email or share. We also record when you follow or unfollow a situation, send feedback, tap Share, or open the weekly email. If you are signed in, the record also carries your account number, so we can tell which signed-in people find the site useful.
  • Not collected: IP address, user agent, the full URL or the search text you type into the place picker. Link previews and crawlers (for example a WhatsApp preview of a shared link) are not counted as visitors.
  • Your choices in cookies. The state or area you pick, and the times of your last visits, are kept in cookies in your browser so the home page can show “since your last visit”. They are sent back to us with each page request, but we do not copy them into our database.

If you use “Use my location”

Only when you press the button, and only after your browser asks your permission, your phone or computer sends its position to us once. We find which local government area and state contain it, show you the answer and discard the position. It is not stored or written to a log.

If you create an account

  • Your email address, when you ask for a sign-in link. An account is created the moment an address is entered, and becomes verified when the link is used. Someone who types your address by mistake or on purpose can make us send you a sign-in email; if you ignore it, nothing else happens.
  • Sign-in records. The link is valid for {{ login_link_minutes }} minutes and works once. You then stay signed in for up to {{ session_days }} days, or until you have not used the site for {{ idle_days }} days, or until you sign out (on this device, or everywhere from your account page). We keep only one-way fingerprints of links and sessions, never the links themselves.
  • What you ask us to remember: the situations you follow, the states and topics you choose, the notifications we have shown you, and whether and when you turned on the weekly email.

If you turn on the weekly email

We email your address on Monday mornings with the situations you follow that changed and the biggest changes in your places. If a situation you follow is corrected or withdrawn we email that too, but only to people who turned the weekly email on. Each email has a link that turns the emails off in one click. The email contains a tiny image that tells us it was opened, and we record that against your account (once per week). That is the only tracking in it.

If you send feedback

  • “Was this useful?” saves your yes or no, the version of the page, and your visitor code.
  • “Report an error” saves what you wrote (up to 2,000 characters), the page version and your visitor code, and your account number if you are signed in. An email address is saved only if you type one in the optional box, and is used only to reply about that report. Please do not put personal details about yourself or other people in the text.

Technical logs

Our own software does not write visitor IP addresses anywhere. To limit abuse we hold your address briefly in memory, scrambled with a code that changes each time the server restarts, and forget it when the time window ends. The companies that run our servers and network may still keep short technical logs that include IP addresses, for security and to keep the service running. {{ todo("which hosting provider and any proxy or CDN are used, what they log, and for how long; set the shortest practical period") }}

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act you can ask us to tell you what we hold about you, give you a copy, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, and withdraw consent. Most of this you can do yourself:

  • Copy of your data: on the account page, “Download everything we hold about you”. It covers your account, choices, follows, notifications, feedback and the page-view records that were recorded while you were signed in. Page views from before you signed in carry only a visitor code and cannot be tied to you, so they are not included.
  • Delete your account: on the same page. It is immediate.
  • Stop the weekly email: the link in any email, or the account page.
  • Anything else, including removing your email address or personal details from a report you sent, or help if you cannot sign in: write to {{ contact() }}.
